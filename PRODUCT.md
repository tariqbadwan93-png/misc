# Product

> Inferred on 2026-11-09 from the misc. brand PDFs, the previous site's copy and the owner's brief
> ("way too busy", closer to monopo.london, gallery like Motion's gallery showcase). Not interviewed; correct anything that is off.

## Register

brand

## Users

People deciding whether to hire misc.: founders launching something new, hospitality groups opening a concept,
brands that need a stand, a site and a story at once, and teams tired of managing five agencies.
They arrive from Instagram on a phone or from a referral on a laptop, and give the site a minute.

## Product Purpose

The studio site for misc., a multi-disciplinary design house (branding, spaces and exhibitions, concept F&B, web,
apps, campaigns). It has to show range and taste in one scroll and end in an email to hello@wearemisc.com.

## Brand Personality

Generalist, confident, wry. "Express, don't impress." Short sentences, plain words, one joke at most.
It should feel calm and expensive, with a single moment of play (the glass asterisk).

## Anti-references

- The first version of this site (git tag `v1-busy`): glyph fields, HUD lines, tickers, readouts, a custom cursor,
  eleven sections. Too much to look at.
- Template agency sites that explain their process in numbered steps and stat counters.
- SaaS landing pages: feature grids, gradient text, purple glow.

## Design Principles

1. One idea per screen. Hero, one sentence, the work, the ask.
2. Show the work. Photography carries the page; copy stays short.
3. Motion is material, not decoration: light, glass and depth respond to the pointer and the scroll.
4. Colour is restrained. A dark teal ground; orange only on the asterisks and in the light.
5. If it doesn't help someone say yes, cut it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text at least 4.5:1 on the dark ground. Reduced motion gets a still scene and instant transitions.
The gallery works by keyboard (cards are buttons; the open project is a modal dialog with Escape, arrows and a focus trap)
and falls back to a plain scrollable list without JavaScript or WebGL.
